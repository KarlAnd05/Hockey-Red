Refunds & Returns

Thirty days to change your mind. Faulty goods always put right.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

In short: you have 30 days from delivery to return unworn, unwashed items with their tags attached. Personalized and custom-made items cannot be returned unless they are faulty. If anything arrives damaged or wrong, we cover it in full.

1. The return window

You may request a return within 30 days of delivery. Contact us before sending anything back so we can give you a return reference.

2. Condition of returned items

To be accepted, an item must be:

  • Unworn, unwashed, and free of odour, marks, or damage;
  • In its original packaging with all tags still attached;
  • Accompanied by your order number.

We may refuse a return, or reduce the refund, where an item has been handled more than was necessary to check its size and fit.

3. Personalized and custom-made items

Items made or altered to your specification are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged unless they are faulty or we made an error. This includes:

  • Official Team Lebanon Hockey Jerseys with a name, number, or other personalization;
  • Any other item customised at your request.

Please check your spelling and size carefully before ordering a personalized item. We confirm the details with you before production wherever possible.

4. Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items

If an item arrives faulty or damaged, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 14 days of delivery and send a photograph. We will arrange a full refund or a replacement, whichever you prefer, and we pay the return shipping. This applies to personalized items too.

5. How to start a return

  1. Email info@lebanonhockey.com with your order number (it looks like LH-2026-00001), the item, and the reason.
  2. We reply with a return reference and the address to send it to.
  3. Pack the item securely with the reference enclosed and send it back.
  4. We inspect it on arrival and confirm your refund by email.

You can find your order number and download your invoice at any time from your account.

6. Return shipping

For a change of mind, you pay the return postage, and we recommend a tracked service — we cannot refund an item that does not reach us. Original shipping charges are not refunded. For faulty or incorrect items, we cover shipping both ways.

7. Refund timing and method

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe. We process refunds within 3 business days of receiving and inspecting the item, and your bank typically takes a further 5 to 10 business days to post it. We cannot refund to a different card or account.

8. Exchanges

Need a different size? Return the original under the rules above and place a new order — that way you secure your size before it sells out. Tell us it is an exchange and we will not charge shipping on the replacement.

9. Items we cannot accept

  • Personalized or custom-made items, unless faulty;
  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery;
  • Items that are worn, washed, damaged, or missing their tags;
  • Gift cards, where offered.

10. Order cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible if you want to cancel. If the order has not entered production or shipping we will cancel it and refund you in full. Personalized items cannot be cancelled once production has begun.

11. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under Quebec's Consumer Protection Act or other applicable consumer law. Those rights apply in addition to the terms above, including where goods do not match their description or are not fit for their ordinary purpose.

Need help with a return? Email info@lebanonhockey.com — we would much rather sort it out than have you stuck with something that does not fit.
