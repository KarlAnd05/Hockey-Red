Women's National Team

Dedication. Passion. Purpose.

Started in 2018 by a group of committed athletes, the Women's program is now recognized by the IIHF and fields both a National Team and a Next Generation Team, with players competing at NCAA Division 1 and 3 schools, U Sports universities, and international leagues.
