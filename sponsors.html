What You Get

Three ways to be seen

Each plan comes in four tiers - Cedar, Gold, Silver and Bronze. The number of partners per tier is capped on purpose: space on a jersey is finite, and a partnership that everyone has is not worth having.

On the uniform

Your mark travels with the national team to every event it plays, at home and abroad.

  • Cedar - front of the official jersey, plus helmet and shorts
  • Gold - sleeve of the official jersey
  • Cedar and Gold - practice jerseys
  • All four tiers - off-ice attire and an invitation to every federation event

$3,500 - $15,000

At the events

Tournaments, training camps, exhibition games and fundraisers, run through the year.

  • Cedar - official sponsor, ceremonial puck drop, logo on promotional items, photos and video
  • Cedar and Gold - game-day posters, digital promotion, poster at the entrance
  • Cedar, Gold and Silver - official programme and social media promotion
  • All four tiers - a booth, tickets and an acknowledgement during the event

$250 - $10,000

Through our channels

The website, the newsletter and the federation's social accounts, reaching people who follow Lebanese hockey closely.

  • Cedar - a banner on this website and a promotional video or documentary
  • Cedar and Gold - a player interview video, a blog, an article about your company
  • Cedar, Gold and Silver - a newsletter article and your logo on the newsletter
  • All four tiers - your logo on this website and mentions on social media

$500 - $6,000

Flexibility

Shaping it around you

Take more than one

Two plans at the same tier take 10% off. Three take 20%. The tiers have to match for the discount to apply.

Support in kind

Products, media support, accommodation, ground and air transport, venues, catering and equipment all count towards the same thresholds as a cash contribution, and are recognised the same way.

Build your own

If none of the three fits, say so. Elements can be added or dropped, or a plan built from scratch around what you actually want out of it.
