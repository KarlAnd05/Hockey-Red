Lebanese Ice Hockey Federation

About Us

Founded to advance hockey opportunities for Lebanese players worldwide and give Lebanon a voice on the international ice hockey stage.

Where We're Headed

Building for the future

A non-profit, mandated by Lebanon's Ministry of Sports and the Lebanese Olympic Committee and recognised by the IIHF as its official representative in Lebanon. The federation runs from Montreal, close to both the diaspora and the North American institutions it works with, while building toward a permanent home for the sport in Lebanon.

Founded May 2018 Ministry of Sports Decree, 2018 (renewed 2022) IIHF Associate Member since 2019 Lebanese Olympic Committee Member since 2020

Beyond The Rink

Hockey as a bridge between communities

At first glance hockey may seem distant from Lebanese roots. That distance is the point: the game belongs to no single community, so it can be shared by all of them. The federation is a non-profit, and it treats fair play, respect, equality and diversity as part of the work - participation open regardless of background or means.

A route into Canadian life

Hockey crosses cultural and linguistic barriers. Young Lebanese players pick up the skills of the sport and, with them, the habits of teamwork, perseverance and respect that carry well beyond it.

Where communities meet

Families gather at the arena and argue happily about the last shift. Lebanese players on local teams become ambassadors for their heritage while taking their own place in the Canadian mosaic.

Integration runs both ways

Canadians who welcome these players get to know a culture they might not otherwise meet, and Lebanese families feel more at home in an adopted country. Neither side does it alone.

Identity & Reach

More than a team sheet

The cedar's colours

The uniform puts the country's cedar front and centre, with the national red and white worked through the details. Home, away and a limited edition - one set of colours, carried onto international ice.

On the big screen

A Radio-Canada documentary directed by Frédéric Nassif, a Quebecer of Lebanese origin, follows a national team drawn from every corner of the world, and looks at immigration and integration through the lens of hockey.

Based in Montreal

Running operations from Montreal keeps the federation close to the diaspora and to the North American coaches, clubs and institutions it works with.

What Comes Next

Development objectives

The federation's plan over three horizons - from getting more people on the ice this season to a rink of Lebanon's own.

Short term

  • Train players in Lebanon and overseas.
  • Recruit and develop coaches, referees and volunteers.
  • Explore natural mountain ice surfaces for winter hockey.
  • Enter more international tournaments.
  • Build on the September 2023 memorandum of understanding with the Municipality of Jounieh.

Medium term

  • Complete a feasibility and business plan for the proposed ice-sport complex.
  • Increase local and international awareness, and corporate support.
  • Run summer camps and exhibition games, potentially in Canada and France, to bring through youth players.

Long term

  • Fund and build a multi-sport venue with an NHL-sized rink, gym, pro shop and food service, with room for related sports and events - the federation's plan puts the arena at 5,000 seats.
  • Establish local development and a Lebanese Hockey League of at least four teams.
  • Enter IIHF competitions and world championships, and ultimately Winter Olympic competition.
