## `/about` — About Us

**NEW in full.** Hockey-Red's about page carries a much shorter version; everything below
is richer or absent.

### Who they are

> The Lebanese Ice Hockey Federation (LIHF), also known as Lebanon Hockey® and/or Hockey
> Lebanon®, is the governing body overseeing the Lebanese Local and international Ice
> Hockey teams and players development programs. Currently based in Montreal, Canada with
> youth operations based in Beirut, Lebanon. The LIHF also manages the Lebanese National
> team at international tournaments, events, and exhibition games.

**NEW:** "youth operations based in Beirut" — Hockey-Red never mentions Beirut operations.

### Milestones (more precise than Hockey-Red's)

- Federation created **May 2018**
- **October 2018** — Lebanese Ministry of Sports decree, renewed **14 July 2022**
- **September 2019** — official Associate member of the IIHF
- **February 2020** — official member of the Lebanese Olympic Committee
- **2021 (NEW)** — elected to the board of the **IIHF Associate Committee Working Group**,
  alongside Argentina, North Macedonia and Ireland, with Mexico and Bosnia-Herzegovina as
  advisors
- **(NEW)** Advanced in evaluating the financial and feasibility studies for the first
  multi-disciplinary ice-rink complex in Lebanon

### President's quote (**NEW**)

> "We still have a long way & tough road ahead of us, but with our collective belief in our
> mission & passion to represent our roots, I have no doubt that we will overcome any
> obstacle" — Charles El-Mir, President

### Our Story (**NEW** in full)

- Men's team's first unofficial outing: a friendly vs **Haiti, May 2017**; then exhibition
  games and club tournaments in **Abu Dhabi (UAE)**, which led to founding the LIHF in May 2018.
- Women's team launched **December 2018 at McGill University**; camps and games in Montreal
  and Ottawa (Aug 2019), Toronto camp and games (Dec 2019), Paris camp and friendship games
  vs the **U-18 French National team** (Aug 2021).
- **May 2023** — Men's National Team won **Gold** at the Kuwait Arab Cup, champions of the
  first Arab Nation Cup.
- **May 2025** — Women's team won **Bronze** at The Dream Nation Cup, **New Jersey**.
- **June 2025** — Men's team won **Bronze** in 3-on-3 at the **Kazan Islamic Tournament**.
- Planned for 2025–26: Challenger Series (Montreal, 10–13 July), **LATAM Cup (Miami)**,
  and **Lebanon-Quebec Challenge (Montreal, 2–4 January 2026)**. Both of the latter are
  **NEW** — Hockey-Red lists neither.

### Mission & Values (**NEW** in full)

Created to advance hockey initiatives of Lebanese-based players worldwide and to enable
international development and representation of Lebanon in the sport. Core value: working
together as one Lebanese family regardless of political and religious affiliation, to shed
a positive light on Lebanon internationally through ice hockey, with those values instilled
in every player and staff member, in the arena and outside it.

### Objectives (**NEW** in full)

**Short-term:** continuously train and develop players in Lebanon and abroad; recruit and
professionally train coaches, referees and volunteers; **natural ice rinks in the mountains**
for 2–3 months of hockey (targeting winter 2025); maximise participation in international
tournaments for exposure; **MOU signed September 2023 for a prime location, Municipality of
Jounieh**.

**Mid-term:** finalise the business plan and feasibility study for the first
multidisciplinary complex in Lebanon; increase exposure of the sport within the Lebanese
community worldwide to grow awareness and corporate support; organise summer training camps
and exhibition games in Canada or France to widen the youth talent pool.

**Long-term:** with private investment, build a multi-sports complex with at least one
NHL-size rink, gym, pro-shop and restaurants, also accommodating figure skating, basketball,
volleyball, ball hockey, inline hockey, events and shows; bring the sport to Lebanon and
develop players and staff from the country of origin; create the **Lebanese Hockey League
(LHL)** with a minimum of four teams; participate in IIHF-sanctioned tournaments and World
Championships; represent Lebanon at the IIHF World Championship and the Winter Olympics.
