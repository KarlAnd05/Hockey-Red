Cookie Policy

We use exactly one cookie, and only when you actually need it.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to store. This page lists every cookie Lebanon Hockey uses and what each one does.

We use one cookie, and no tracking of any kind. There are no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no social media pixels, and no third-party scripts on this site.

The cookie we use

NamePurposeSet whenExpiresType
lh_sid Identifies your browsing session so we can remember what is in your cart, keep you signed in, and protect forms against cross-site request forgery. Only when you first add something to the cart, sign in, register, or submit a form. Simply reading pages sets no cookie at all. 30 days, or immediately when you sign out First-party, strictly necessary, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax

The cookie holds a random identifier only. It contains no personal information, and it cannot be read by JavaScript or by any other website.

Why there is no cookie banner

Consent banners exist because of cookies that track you. Ours does not: lh_sid is strictly necessary to deliver a service you have explicitly asked for — keeping your cart, or keeping you signed in — and cookies of that kind are recognised as exempt from prior consent under Quebec's Law 25 and comparable rules elsewhere.

We have also deliberately delayed it: the cookie is issued only at the moment you do something that needs it. If you browse the site and never use the cart or sign in, no cookie is ever set.

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookie, we will ask for your consent first and this page will change.

Cookies set by Stripe during payment

When you check out, we send you to Stripe's own hosted payment page at checkout.stripe.com. Stripe sets its own cookies there for fraud prevention and to operate that page. Those cookies belong to Stripe, are set on Stripe's domain, and are governed by Stripe's privacy policy — we cannot read them. You are returned to our site once payment completes.

Similar technologies

The shop uses your browser's sessionStorage to remember a discount code you have typed while you move between the cart and checkout. That information stays on your device, is cleared when you close the tab, and is never sent anywhere except when you apply the code.

If you start a chat with us, your browser's localStorage keeps one value, lh_chat_token, so your conversation is still there as you move from page to page or come back later. It identifies the conversation and nothing else — it is not an account, it is not linked to your name unless you tell us, and it is not used to follow you around the site. Clearing your browser storage ends that conversation; you can always start a new one.

Chat messages are kept so the federation can answer you and refer back to what was asked. They are held for up to 12 months, the same as messages sent through the contact form, and then deleted.

Controlling cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings, usually under Privacy or Site Data. Blocking lh_sid will stop the shopping cart and sign-in from working, because the site has no other way to recognise your session. The rest of the site will still work normally.

Changes

If the cookies we use change, we will update this page and the date shown at the top.
